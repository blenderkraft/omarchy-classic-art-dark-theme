# Classic Art Dark

A dark Omarchy theme built from the most intense, high-drama paintings in art
history: beheadings, shipwrecks, storms, nightmares, and the apocalypse.
Each picture sits centered on a 2560x1440 canvas with black bars, so the
painting stays whole — nothing is cropped. The desktop is museum-wall
charcoal, with a chiaroscuro venetian-red accent and antique gilt-gold
complement.

The artists here died well over 70 years ago (Fuseli 1825, Blake 1827,
Friedrich 1840, Goya 1828, Gericault 1824, Delacroix 1863, Rembrandt 1669,
Caravaggio 1610, Martin 1854, Bocklin 1901, Aivazovsky 1900, Vereshchagin
1904, Repin 1930, Munch 1944, von Stuck 1928), so their paintings are
squarely public domain worldwide. Gentileschi's Judith Slaying Holofernes
photo is a CC0 museum release, and the Caravaggio Judith and the Fuseli
Nightmare photos carry CC BY-SA 4.0 / CC BY 2.5 photographer licenses on the
2-D photographic reproduction of a public-domain painting — permissive
either way. Sources are Wikimedia Commons; the photographers/uploaders of
record are credited on each file's Commons page.
